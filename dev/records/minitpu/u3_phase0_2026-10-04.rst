Phase 0 gives each U3 unit an oracle: an ``RtlUnit`` spec, stimulus, and a
numpy reference held bit-exact against the Verilator-wrapped ``.sv`` on every
defined slot, with every declared latency measured by a step probe, and
MiniTPU's own tbs run unchanged as the second check (D-7). For the MXU the
reference is the **push / commit / pop contract**, not a model of ``mxu.sv``.

Reproduce (``source examples/minitpu/harness/env-zhang21.sh`` first)::

   $ALLO_PYTHON -m examples.minitpu.harness.characterize sfu xlu_reduction_tree xlu_transpose   # ~35 s warm
   $ALLO_PYTHON -m examples.minitpu.harness.characterize mxu_pe mxu_systolic_array mxu        # ~3 min warm

Outputs: ``u3_phase0_2026-10-04/characterize_xlu_sfu.log``,
``characterize_mxu.log``; the tbs: ``tbs_xlu_sfu.log``, ``tbs_mxu.log``.

The span is measured without the assertion: refill the bank of a tile that is
still switching at offsets around the span, and take the smallest offset from
which every larger offset leaves the tile exact (the monotone-suffix rule of
``tb_vpu_latency_probe``). At DIM 16 offsets 71-74 corrupt the tile and 75-77
do not; ``mxu.sv:259`` fires at exactly 74.

The references
==============

**``sfu``** (``ref.sfu``). A bit model of ``sfu.sv``: ``fp32_abs_q7`` with
saturation, the GELU/EXP address fold, ROM lookup, the PWL decode
(``bias - product[19:11]``, truncated to 7 mantissa bits), the ``rsqrt``
exponent, and every special case. The two ``.mem`` ROMs and the two 32-entry
case tables are **data read from the clone** (the tables parsed out of
``sfu.sv``): a wrong table passes here, and ``tb_sfu_math_sweep`` (which
computes ``exp``/erf-GELU itself) is the check that covers them. Against the
exact functions rounded RNE the RTL differs on 278,601 vectors, all
classified (``characterize_xlu_sfu.log``): PWL truncation <= 2 ulp
(73,794); ``vrsqrt(x<0)`` is ``+0x7fc0`` (57,569); GELU and EXP table steps
(57,023, 27,856); ``vexp(x>0) = 1.0`` (30,687); domain clamps (27,969, 548);
canonical NaN (866); subnormal operands (707, 702); the ``vrecip`` top
binades (450, 423; finding 1); ``vrsqrt(+-0)`` NaN (7).

**``xlu_reduction_tree``** (``ref.xlu_reduction_tree_trace``). Pairs
``(2e, 2e+1)`` per level, ``vpu_bf16_add`` (a = left) or the ``bf16_gt`` max
(ties to ``b``), op per wavefront. The adders' payload is ungated, so the
result is a function of the inputs ``2*LEVELS + 1`` edges earlier on **every**
cycle: valids are held every cycle, results on valid cycles, and the "no valid"
census shows the guess matched every one of its 47,432 slots.

**``xlu_transpose``** (``ref.xlu_transpose_trace``). Whole-row writes;
registered crossing read ``out[s][l] = tile[l][4*idx + s]``; the tile is
never reset (a read of an unwritten row is ``uninit``, and all 4 such slots
moved with the seed).

**``mxu_pe`` and ``mxu_systolic_array``** (``ref_mxu``, cycle models). Every
register of ``mxu_pe.sv``; the array is a grid of them wired as the ``.sv``
wires it (row 0's ``psum_in = '0`` with ``psum_valid = lhs_valid``; the
weight-valid broadcast down each column). Unreset payload carries a taint:
``uninit`` until known, ``reset fill`` for an adder output whose special-class
stage was forced by reset while its payload kept flowing -- 21 of the PE's 338
such slots differ from the data function, so the mask is needed.

**``mxu``: the contract** (``ref_mxu.mxu_trace``). No ``mxu.sv`` register is
modelled. What a client may rely on:

1. A word pushed in cycle ``p`` is accepted in ``p + 1`` (the input FIFO
   drains every cycle, so it can never overflow: a full FIFO is also popping).
2. An RHS word shifts into pending bank ``load_bank``: row 0 takes it, row
   ``r`` takes row ``r-1``'s, so the first of DIM beats lands in the bottom row.
3. ``weight_commit_i`` toggles ``load_bank`` and makes the bank just loaded
   the next tile's; the next LHS word starts that tile, and every activation of
   the tile is multiplied by that bank as it stood at the tile's start.
4. Activation ``a`` (lane ``r`` -> row ``r``) gives per lane ``c``
   ``pack_bf16`` of ``psum_r = acc24_add(a_r * W[r][c], psum_{r-1})``,
   ``psum_{-1} = +0``, ascending ``r``.
5. Results gather per lane in groups of ``NUM_SUBLANES`` LHS words, counted
   globally (a group may straddle a reload); lane ``c``'s group enters its
   FIFO ``1 + DIM*PE + c`` cycles after its last word is accepted; a full
   lane FIFO **drops** it (``mxu.sv:44``); ``output_valid_o`` is the AND of
   lane non-empty; a pop while valid removes one group from every lane, a pop
   while not valid is ignored.
6. A refill of a started tile's bank less than the span after the start
   breaks that tile (masked ``bank overwrite``).

Programs are laid out as the stream and pop engines lay them out (a load is
DIM rows back to back with the commit in the cycle the last is accepted; a
push is 4 rows back to back; a pop either fires once where valid, or is held
from before valid as a waiting ``vmatpop``), and legal ones keep the switch
span and the 16-group capacity. Directed: ``identity`` (the tb's scenario),
``banks-at-span`` (a refill at exactly the span), ``fifo-full-exact``,
``waiting-pops``, ``group-straddles-load``, ``push-before-load`` (zero
weights after reset), ``reset-mid-stream``; illegal: ``overflow`` (the 17th
group dropped on every lane, DIM ``vpu_fifo`` overflow assertions, the
reference's drop model exact), ``bank-overwrite`` (one assertion; the tile's
slot masked, and the reference's clean guess **does not** match: the
corruption is real), ``commit-early`` (the last RHS row goes to the other
bank: defined, and silent at the ``mxu`` boundary -- only the stream engine
asserts it).

Findings
========

1. **MiniTPU, ``vrecip`` on the top binades** (FYI, P-10). For
   ``|x|`` in ``[2^127, 2^128)`` the exponent ``253 - 254`` wraps to ``0xff``:
   Inf or a **non-canonical NaN** pattern (``0x7f00`` -> ``0x7fff``), against
   ``ARITHMETIC.md`` §6 ("every unit emits the same canonical NaN"). For
   ``[2^126, 2^127)`` the result has exponent 0 and loses its hidden bit (worth
   half).
2. **MiniTPU, no subnormal flush in the SFU**, unlike both multipliers:
   ``vrecip(0x0001) = 0x7efe`` (IEEE: Inf); GELU/EXP send a subnormal to the
   centre bin.
3. ``vrsqrt(+-0)`` is NaN (documented, ``x <= 0``; IEEE ``+-Inf``).
4. **MiniTPU elaborates and is bit-exact at N = 16** (``xlu_reduction_tree``,
   ``MINITPU_NUM_LANES=4``), though ``UNITS.md`` §1 says no geometry but 16x4
   was ever built; its tree tbs are **not** geometry-generic
   (``tb_reduce_pipe.sv:157`` hard-codes ``16'h4280``; ``tb_xlu_lane_tap``
   assumes 16 lanes per sublane), so they fail there while the RTL is right.
5. **Payload is unreset throughout U3** (tree, SFU stages, transpose tile,
   PE payload and the adder's): outputs on non-valid cycles follow the data.
   This is D-14's ``reset=False`` on every payload register (plan P-9).
6. **The MXU contract needs no internal register and one derived constant**,
   ``push -> valid = 2 + DIM*(MXU_PE_LATENCY + 1)``, reconciled with
   ``isa_latency.json``'s 85 (4 stream rows + 82 - 1). The array alone has no
   matrix contract (the skew is ``mxu.sv``'s), which is what makes a
   Stream-linked Allo array acceptable at ``mxu`` level (plan P-2).
7. **The weight-switch span is exact**, by function and by assertion
   (5 / 15 / 75): a refill one cycle early corrupts exactly the last PE.
8. **Method: random data hides a one-PE corruption.** The first span probe
   read 74 at DIM 16 with random weights: one wrong weight of 256 rounded away
   in the bf16 pack. A probe of a contract that can fail in one PE needs data
   built so that PE decides the output.
9. The output FIFO's **drop** (u3_fifo_composed M5) is reproduced at the
   ``mxu`` boundary: the 17th unpopped group is lost on every lane, with one
   ``vpu_fifo`` assertion per lane, and the contract reference predicts it.
10. ``commit-early`` is silent at the ``mxu`` boundary (only
    ``mxu_stream_engine.sv:99`` asserts the commit/last-row relation): a
    contract an Allo ``mxu`` unit must state, since nothing below checks it.

Not done in Phase 0
