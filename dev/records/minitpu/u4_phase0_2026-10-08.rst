Phase 0 gives each U4 unit an oracle: an ``RtlUnit`` spec (``VARIANTS = {}``),
stimulus (directed, random, MiniTPU's assembled programs and images, and
MiniTPU's tbs replayed through their VCD), and a Python reference held
bit-exact against the Verilator-wrapped ``.sv`` on every defined slot, with
every declared latency measured by a step probe and MiniTPU's own tbs run
unchanged as the second check (D-7). Three parallel tracks wrote the parts
(F1 fetch and loop, F2 decode and address, F3 DMA); the integrator wrote the
whole sequencer, the VPU's writeback and the calendar comparison.

Reproduce (``source examples/minitpu/harness/env-zhang21.sh`` first)::

   $ALLO_PYTHON -m examples.minitpu.harness.characterize fetch loop_ctrl              # ~30 s warm
   $ALLO_PYTHON -m examples.minitpu.harness.characterize seq_decoder agu_resolve scalar_agu dma_desc_adapter vpu_adapter
   $ALLO_PYTHON -m examples.minitpu.units.seq_decoder --asm-check
   $ALLO_PYTHON -m examples.minitpu.harness.characterize dma_addr_gen dma
   $ALLO_PYTHON -m examples.minitpu.harness.characterize sequencer vpu_writeback   # vpu build ~2.5 min cold
   $ALLO_PYTHON -m examples.minitpu.harness.calendar                                 # ~3 min warm

The write-port calendar, measured (the minitpu-comp seam)
=========================================================

``vpu.sv`` ORs six writeback sources onto the one VREG write port
(``:289-305``, ``:352-365``), registers the result twice (``wb_stage_*``, then
per sublane ``wb_local_*``), and guards it only with ``$onehot0`` under
``ifndef SYNTHESIS`` (``:370``). The claim cycle ``W`` of each op is what three
copies book: ``asm.py`` (``W_*`` constants, ``_decode``'s write spans,
``_Timeline.write_port``), ``docs/isa_latency.json`` (``rtl_params`` and each
operation's ``w``) and ``sequencer_pkg.sv`` (``WB_W_* = VPU_*_LATENCY +
VPU_WB_STAGES``, booked by the sim-only checker ``sequencer.sv:277-432``).
``calendar.py`` measured it on the RTL three ways per class: the cycle the
source drives the mux (``L``), the cycle the VREG write enable is high
(``W``), and by function, the first cycle VREG port A returns the new value:

**W = unit latency + VPU_WB_STAGES (2) holds for every class, and equals all
three written copies**: the first measurement of the seam agreed with
``minitpu-comp`` (``u1_matrix.rst``, 2026-10-08). ``W`` counts clock cycles
from the issuing bundle (v1-course), and the first legal consumer is
``W + 1`` by function, which is ``isa_latency.json``'s "L = W + 1". The
``vmatpop`` row holds only for a pop issued at least 85 cycles after its push
(``matrix.result_latency``); an earlier pop's beat fires at ``push + 86``, so
its write lands later than ``W = 3`` booked -- the reference predicts it
(``ref_ctrl_wb.pop_mux_cycles``) and the ``matrix-pops`` trace matches.

**Pairs.** Every ordered pair of the six fixed-offset classes at every issue
distance 0..16 (586 cases, each its own trace, ``vpu_writeback``'s fields
random): two writes merge into one write-enable cycle in exactly the 15
cases ``W_a = d + W_b``, which are exactly the 15 cases ``asm._Timeline``
refuses. On the sequencer (806 cases, ``vmatpop`` included, bundles with
``delay = d - 1``): the rule and ``asm`` give 22; the sequencer's own checker
fires on 15 (finding F-W2).

``vpu_ctrl_t`` as the interface
===============================

No narrower seam exists between the sequencer and the VPU: ``vpu_ctrl_t``
(``vpu_pkg.sv:148-181``, 85 bits, 29 fields counting ``vmem``'s four) is the
whole contract, and it is **combinational** from the fetch head
(``sequencer_vpu_adapter``, ``sequencer.sv:467-484``): valid in the issue
cycle only.

* **Producer** (F2, ``vpu_adapter``): nine valid bits are gated by
  ``run_accept`` -- ``alu``, ``txin``, ``txout``, ``sfu``, ``reduce``,
  ``vmem``, ``vmatload``, ``vmatpush``, ``vmatpop``. Every other field is
  ungated payload that follows the fetch head even on stall cycles (12,423
  such rows checked): ``raddr_a/b``, the ``*_vd`` and ``*_op`` fields,
  ``txin/txout_index``, ``reduce_lane``, ``vmem.op/vreg_idx/vmem_address``
  (the address from ``agu_resolve``), ``vmem_store_read_hint`` (read by
  nothing), and ``m.reg_idx`` on all three ``vmat*`` fields. ``alu_op`` is
  cast from 3 to 4 bits: ``vpu_alu``'s AND/OR/XOR are unreachable.
* **Consumer** (this record): every field is sampled in the cycle its op's
  valid is high and never again -- the ALU, SFU and tree register their
  operands then; the tag pipelines carry the ``*_vd`` (they shift every cycle,
  ungated, and only the valid at the end matters); ``vpu_vmem_simd`` registers
  the request and port C's store data then (``:40-46``); the matrix engines
  latch the command and stream port C from ``t + 1``.
  ``vpu_writeback`` randomises every non-valid field on every cycle and
  every field of the ops not issued, and the calendar matches; the data-level
  check (``vpu_ctrl_gating.log``) runs one valid-command stream under two
  random payloads and ends with **equal VREG files (32/32) and equal stored
  VMEM (260/260 beats) on five seeds**, while the control (one destination
  field changed) differs (1/32, 132/260). Matrix commands are not in that
  check.
* **What couples the fields** is resources, not timing: ``raddr_a``/``raddr_b``
  are shared by whichever V op issues (one per bundle); VREG port C is shared
  by a store and a streaming ``vmatload``/``vmatpush``, and the matrix read
  wins silently (``vpu.sv:430``); the one write port is the calendar above;
  ``matrix_busy_o`` is advisory (nothing stalls).

So ``vpu_ctrl_t`` is a set of **valid-qualified, single-cycle, no-backpressure
commands** on four slots (V, X, M, and nothing else), whose only interactions
are the four shared resources -- the declared-interface candidate the plan
puts to the owner (O1).

DMA <-> VMEM port contract (F3)
===============================

One 32 B beat per access on a 16-bit beat pointer (VMEM uses 14 bits,
unchecked); four beats a 128 B word. Reads return ``VMEM_DMA_READ_LATENCY`` = 2
cycles after ``vmem_rd_en``; ``vpu_dma_group`` fetches the word on beat 0 and
serves beats 1-3 from a register, so a word's beats are read in order from 0.
A write commits on beat 3 (``flush_i`` tied 0): a partial word is never
written. ``vmem_gnt`` is tied 1 (``minitpu_core.sv``); inside ``dma.sv`` a
load drain outranks a store read. A same-word compute/DMA collision is
undefined and an obligation (MiniTPU issue #21, D-12's ``collision=``). The
DMA side is one D-12 ``rw`` port of read latency 2 on VMEM, beside the compute
port's ``rw`` of latency 3 (``VMEM_READ_LATENCY``).

Findings
========

MiniTPU (FYI; not filed while the owner is away, as U3's P-10):

* **F-W1. ``vpu.sv``'s ``$onehot0`` cannot see a ``vredsum`` + ``vlanered``
  collision.** The tree's two outputs share writeback source bit 3
  (``vpu.sv:291``), so a ``vredsum`` at ``t`` and a ``vlanered`` at ``t + 4``
  (both write at ``t + 15``) merge silently: one write to ``vd_a | vd_b`` of
  OR-ed data, neither destination written, no assertion (8 such cycles in
  the unfiltered random stream). ``asm.py`` and the sequencer's checker do
  refuse it.
* **F-W2. The sequencer's sim-only calendar never books ``vtxout``.**
  ``candidate_e_writes`` (``sequencer.sv:298``) omits ``txout_valid``, so 7 of
  the 22 colliding pairs (``vtxout`` against ALU, SFU, both reductions,
  ``vld``, ``vmatpop``) pass its write-port check; ``vpu.sv``'s assertion
  catches six of them (different sources). ``sequencer_pkg.sv:7`` documents
  the RAW/WAW exclusion, not this one.
* **F-W3. A lost release.** The checker's release pipeline holds one address
  per slot (``sequencer.sv:357-388``); two writes landing in one cycle
  overwrite each other's slot, and the loser's VREG stays pending, so a legal
  read many cycles later asserts RAW. Only after an illegal collision; the
  reference models it register for register and predicts all six RAW
  assertions of the ``unscheduled`` trace.
* **F-W4. ``schedule()`` accepts a back-edge WAW that the sequencer asserts.**
  A ``loop.begin.r`` body that loads one VREG per pass, scheduled by
  ``asm.schedule()``, fires two "WAW remains" assertions; MiniTPU's
  ``tools/check_back_edges.py`` flags the same bundle twice. Documented
  (``isa_latency.json`` scheduler.not_modelled), and the reason
  ``check_back_edges`` is a separate gate.
* Loop-stack overflow (a ninth nested loop) and underflow are silent in
  hardware (flags left open, ``sequencer.sv:163``; no assertion); a ninth
  ``loop.begin`` still resets the loop buffer (F1).
* S ops 5-7 decode as valid, claim the IMM and write 0 after ``S_LAT``;
  ``asm.py``'s ``_S_IMM_OPS`` disagrees on them; a descriptor channel bit
  above ``DMA_CHANNEL_SEL_W`` is dropped (F2). Neither occurs in assembled
  code: on 5,255 real bundles ``asm.py``'s decode agrees with the RTL's on
  every field it reads; on random words its reads are a superset (safe).
* A channel reused without a clear hangs (sim-only assertion only); a DMA
  word address above 2^29 is truncated and a stride-0 store is accepted; a
  bridge that skips a burst attributes later data to the wrong rows until the
  watchdog fires (F3).

Harness:

* **H1. Verilator's ``--x-initial unique`` gives every entry of an unpacked
  array the same random value** (all 32 VREGs read equal at power-up, per
  seed). A read of the wrong register is then invisible; the VPU probes
  preload distinct contents through the DMA port first. U2's masks are
  unaffected (uninitialised slots were masked, and differ across seeds).
* **H2. Assertion time stamps are not cycles** for sources with
  ``timescale 1ns/1ps`` (``rtl.py`` sets ``ctx->time(t + 1)`` in the
  precision unit; ``$error`` prints ``[0]``). The pair sweeps therefore run
  one case per trace. Fix: scale the time by the timescale.
* A trace starting with ``rst_n`` low leaves an async-reset unit undefined in
  row 0 (no edge yet): masked as reset. ``vcd_seed.extract`` reads one scope
  and needs ``PASS <tb>``; ``seed_rows`` (F2) and two local extractors (F1,
  F3) work around both without changing the harness.
