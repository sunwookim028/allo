Facts the plan rests on [V]:

* **Payload is unreset everywhere in U3; only valids, ops and commits reset**
  (tree leaves and levels, SFU stages, transpose tile, PE ``lhs``/``product``/
  ``psum`` and the adder payload). Outputs on non-valid cycles still follow the
  data (census "no valid": the guess matched every tree slot), so verdicts hold
  outputs on valid cycles only.
* **The MXU is a contract plus one timing constant.** A client may rely on: a
  push accepted next cycle; ``DIM`` RHS rows shift into ``load_bank``
  bottom-first; a commit makes the bank just loaded the next tile's; each LHS
  row gives ``pack_bf16`` of the acc24 chain in ascending row order; groups of
  ``NUM_SUBLANES`` rows per lane; ``output_valid`` = AND of lane non-empty,
  ``2 + DIM*(PE+1)`` after a group's last push; a full lane FIFO **drops**;
  a refill earlier than the span corrupts the switching tile. Everything else
  in ``mxu.sv`` (skew lines, commit-bank skew, gather registers) is
  implementation, free to change.
* **The array alone has no matrix contract**: the skew lives in ``mxu.sv``. It
  is a cycle-locked grid; only a cycle-locked Allo form can be held to its
  cycle model.
* **MiniTPU elaborates at N = 16** and is bit-exact there, though ``UNITS.md``
  says no geometry but 16x4 was ever built; its tree tbs are not geometry-generic.

2. Allo expression options per unit
-----------------------------------

Decisions that apply to every unit: **D-10** (a backend reports latency;
``latency=L`` only where a contract demands it), **D-13** (comb outputs
declared), **D-14** (``Stateful(..., reset=False)`` for unreset storage),
**D-12** (ported memories), **D-11** (single-call traces until verdicts need
more), checkpoint 2 item 3 (NaN sign/payload and the zero sign are
don't-care), checkpoint 6 (pipe-as-data is the reference form of a registered
pipeline). U1 gives the arithmetic: ``bf16_add.bits``,
``mul_acc24.bits``, ``acc24_add_pipe.bits``/``staged``, ``alu.bits`` (and
``bf16_gt``), all bit-exact on simulator, csim and Catapult RTL.

**sfu.** Which contract: 5 cycles is consumed by the sequencer (``WB_W_SFU`` 7).

* **S1 ``bits``** (default): ``sfu.sv`` transcribed into one kernel per element:
  ``fp32_abs_q7`` with saturation, the address fold, two 2,048-entry ROMs,
  the 32-entry PWL tables, truncating decode. ROMs as module-level constant
  arrays loaded from the ``.mem`` files at build. Probes: how each backend
  lowers a 2,048 x 16 b constant (Catapult ROM vs logic; Vitis BRAM; RTLGen;
  AMC ``rom_1r``), and the C4 path (a module-level numpy array -- fixed on
  ``main``, worth a regression here).
* **S2 ``staged``**: S1 with the five registers written as data (pipe-as-data),
  for the cycle-equal Catapult cell.
* ``sfu_group`` is ``LANES`` instances sharing ``valid``/``op``: an instance
  count, i.e. C9 (sized at instantiation).

**xlu_reduction_tree.** Contract: 13 / tap 9 (``WB_W_REDUCE`` 15, ``WB_W_LANE_REDUCE`` 11).

* **T1 ``bits``** (reference): one kernel, ``log2 N`` levels of
  ``bf16_add.bits`` / ``bf16_gt`` select on a per-wavefront op tag, the tap at
  level ``log2 NUM_LANES``. Untimed on the simulator; Catapult picks the depth.
* **T2 ``units``** (the composition probe): ``N - 1`` adder *units*
  (``bf16_add_pipe``) wired by level, plus the 2-deep max path. MiniTPU assumes
  the adder's 2 cycles in two places and checks it nowhere; here the max delay
  is **derived from the adder unit's declared latency** and the tap level from
  ``NUM_LANES`` -- legality conditions, as ``ip/units/reduction_tree.py``
  already does (``ip_gaps.rst``). This is also the **adder-tree matrix engine**
  of the swap (section 3).
* **T3 ``library``**: TinyTPU's ``reduction_tree.py`` with a bf16 node engine.
  Its legality refuses a node type narrower than ``IN + DEPTH``; a bf16 tree
  *rounds at every level by design*, so the library must say "rounding tree,
  order declared" instead of refusing -- the first test of whether the library
  generalises from integers.

**xlu_transpose.** Contract: read 1 (``WB_W_TXOUT`` 3).

* **X1 ``trace``** (default): one kernel; ``tile @ Stateful(reset=False)``
  16 x 16 x ``UInt(16)``; a write lands four rows; the crossing read is
  registered (pipe-as-data, latency 1).
* **X2 ``ported``**: D-12 does **not** describe it: the read is a permutation
  across every row, not a port on a word. Probe whether ``Memory(ports=...)``
  can say it at all, or whether a register array plus a registered mux is the
  only honest form (hypothesis H13).

**mxu_pe.** Contract: ``MXU_PE_LATENCY`` = 4, which ``mxu.sv``'s skew depends on (asserted).

* **P1 ``bits``**: one kernel per PE: ``mul_acc24.bits`` on (lhs, active),
  ``acc24_add_pipe.staged``, two pending banks and the active weight as
  registers, forwards as registers. Checked per cycle against
  ``mxu_pe_trace``.
* **P2 ``unit``**: P1 as a ``compose.unit`` with stream ports (lhs/commit
  east, weights/psum south), the TinyTPU ``pe`` shape. The **MAC plug-in**
  is its pair ``(mul, add)`` plus their types: bf16 -> acc24 here, int8 ->
  int32 in TinyTPU.

**mxu_systolic_array.**

* **A1 ``streams``**: ``mapping=[DIM, DIM]`` of P2 over ``Stream`` links
  (TinyTPU's form). Self-timed: tokens align by order, so the skew is
  unnecessary and the cycle model cannot hold (each link is a FIFO, 3 cycles
  push->pop in Catapult, ``u3_fifo_composed``). Judged at ``mxu`` on the
  contract.
* **A2 ``locked``**: P1 instances over ``Wire`` links, cycle-locked like the
  RTL, so ``mxu_systolic_array_trace`` applies on Catapult RTL. Needs ``Wire``
  *unit* ports (still refused: checkpoint 7) and a pinned per-PE latency on a
  Wire kernel (N2: cannot be pinned today). Expected **blocked**; the probe
  says how far.

**mxu.** Contract: ``mxu_trace``; the timing constant consumed by asm is 85.

* **M1 ``composed``** (default, per ``u3_fifo_composed``): input
  ``Stream[UInt(1+16*DIM), 4]`` consumed by ``try_get`` every cycle; load/skew
  kernel (the skew as pipe-as-data, or dropped under A1); the array; per-lane
  gather + ``pack_bf16`` with ``try_put`` into ``Stream[UInt(16*SUB), 16]``
  (overflow = drop, M5); pop by blocking ``get`` gated on every lane's
  ``empty()``. Reset is the region's.
* **M2 ``microarch``** (``microarch.py``): retired as an oracle. Its acc24 is
  float32 ``round24``, which double-rounds (``ARITHMETIC.md`` §7: 0.06 % of
  adds, one ulp); keep it only as the whole-core model until U5.

3. Composition questions
------------------------

**Q1. The MAC plug-in.** C1/C2 are fixed on ``main`` (``core-scoping``):
an engine passed as a value links and two engines with one def name coexist.
Open: C9 (a ``@df.unit``'s sizes freeze at decoration) and C10 (a function's
schedule does not travel; the tree's 63 adders each need
``leading_zeros17`` unrolled). ``compose.unit`` binds free names when the
``Architecture`` emits, so it does not have C9, and its ``Directives`` carry a
unit's schedule, so composed units do not have C10 (``tinytpu_library.rst``).
Test: one PE source, two MACs (bf16->acc24, int8->int32), each in its
own region and both in one region.

**Q2. The matrix engine: systolic or adder tree.** The two are **different
functions** at bf16: systolic accumulates sequentially in acc24 and rounds once
to bf16 (``ARITHMETIC.md`` §8); a tree rounds at every level in its node type.
So a swap is legal only if the engine interface declares its accumulate order
and node type, and the contract reference takes them as parameters. The timing
constant changes too (linear in DIM against ``log2``): it must come from the
manifest (D-10), never a constant in asm. Test: ``mxu_trace`` with
``order=sequential|tree``; the tree engine is T2 in a ``DotTree``-shaped MXU.

**Q3. Optional modules** (SFU, transpose, reduction tap, TinyTPU's accumulator
file). Mechanism today: a Python conditional over ``Architecture.units()`` and
its channels. Test: a VPU-lane composite with and without the SFU; check that
an unused channel or a dangling ISA slot is refused by the netlist rules, not
left silently unconnected.

**Q4. Parameters and what is derived.** ``DIM``, ``NUM_LANES``,
``NUM_SUBLANES``, ``N``, banks, FIFO depths. Derived, never declared: tree
``LEVELS`` and tap level; the max-path delay (= adder latency); the span
``(DIM-1)*PE + (DIM-1)``; ``push->valid``. Each derived relation becomes a
``legality`` condition on the unit (the ``ip_gaps`` pattern), not an assertion
in a testbench (MiniTPU's tap/booking relation lived only in a tb).

**Q5. Cycle-locked vs self-timed composition.** U3 is where D-10's
"cycle-locked composition" bites: the PE's 4 and the array's skew are a
contract between units. Either the composition is locked (A2: ``latency=`` on
every unit, Wire links) or it is self-timed (A1: Streams) and the contract is
moved up to ``mxu``'s interface, where Phase 0 shows it can be stated without
any internal timing.

5. Order of work and agent split
--------------------------------

Every track works on its own ``u3-*`` branch from ``u3-phase0`` in its own
worktree, writes only its own ``units/*.py`` variants and its own record, and
holds each variant to the Phase 0 oracle with ``check.py`` (one
``UNIT-MATCH`` line per unit/variant/backend). Verilator builds share a cache:
one track per unit. The integrator (one session) is the only writer of
``u3_matrix.rst`` and of ``u1-pilot``.

**Wave 1 (parallel, now):**

* **A -- leaves and XLU** (``u3-xlu-sfu``): S1, S2, T1, X1 on Sim and SC,
  then T2 (units, derived legality). Hypotheses H1, H3, H5, H12. Output:
  the reference expressions and their ``check`` lines.
* **B -- matrix** (``u3-mxu``): P1, P2 on Sim and SC against ``mxu_pe_trace``;
  A1 array; M1 composite against ``mxu_trace`` (directed + random programs,
  every instance). H6, H7, H9, H12.
* **E -- composition design** (``u3-compose``, mostly writing): the engine
  interface (types, latency, accumulate order), unit parameters at
  instantiation (C9), schedules that travel (C10), optional modules (Q3), and
  the derived-parameter legality conditions (Q4), each as a D-n **draft**
  with a minimal prototype outside ``allo/`` (in ``examples/minitpu/``) where
  one is possible. Q1/Q2/Q3 experiments once A's T2 and B's P2 exist (H10,
  H11, H15).

**Wave 2 (as variants land):**

* **C -- Catapult + DC** (``u3-catapult``): every landed ``bits``/``staged``
  variant through SystemC -> Catapult at 3.33 ns and 2.0 ns, RTL in Verilator
  against the oracle per cycle where the form is cycle-locked, ``latency.json``
  against the declared value, same-flow DC area beside MiniTPU's unit (U1
  ``dc_u1.tcl``). Then A2 (H8), ROMs (H2), H4, H14.
* **D -- open HLS** (``u3-openhls``): RTLGen and AMC on S1, T1, P1 (H1, H2,
  H6 columns); AMC's ``rom_1r`` against the SFU tables.

**Wave 3:** the integrator fills ``u3_matrix.rst``, triages, and drafts the
D-n entries E produced for the owner's return.

Dependencies: B's M1 needs P1/A1; C waits on a variant, not a track; E's Q2
needs T2 (A) and P2 (B). Expected cost: A and B about a day each of agent
time; C about a day; D half a day; E a day of writing.

6. Provisional decisions (the owner is away; work proceeds on these)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Each is the agent's call under D-9, with its reason; each stays open for the
owner's review.

* **P-1. Expressions.** Defaults: S1 (+S2 for Catapult), T1 (+T2 as the
  composition probe), X1, P1 -> P2, A1 (A2 as a probe only), M1. *Reason:*
  each default is the form that already matched in U1/U2 or that
  ``u3_fifo_composed`` measured; the others are probes.
* **P-2. Verdicts for composites.** ``mxu`` is judged on the contract trace;
  ``mxu_pe``/``mxu_systolic_array`` on the cycle model only for cycle-locked
  RTL (Catapult). A Stream-linked array gets a contract verdict at ``mxu`` and
  a recorded semantic mismatch per cycle. *Reason:* the array has no matrix
  contract of its own (section 1), and checkpoint 6 already moved FIFOs to
  Streams for the composed MXU.
* **P-3. Latency.** Report first (D-10). Pin with ``latency=`` only where a
  MiniTPU contract consumes the number: PE 4 (the skew), SFU 5, tree 13/9.
  ``mxu``'s push->valid is reported and compared with 82, and its difference
  (Streams: 3 cycles per link) is recorded, not forced. *Reason:* D-10's
  text; the 85 is consumed by asm, so a deviation goes into the manifest the
  assembler would read, not into the source.
* **P-4. Geometry.** Verdicts at the small geometry (tree N=16, array and
  ``mxu`` DIM=4) with the shipped one (N=64, DIM=16) run once per variant.
  The transpose only at 16 x 4. *Reason:* U2 Q2 precedent; Phase 0 shows
  MiniTPU is bit-exact at N=16.
* **P-5. Numerics.** NaN sign/payload and zero sign stay don't-care
  (checkpoint 2); the **MXU accumulate order is not**: sequential acc24 with
  one ``pack_bf16`` must be bit-exact. acc24 only through U1's integer units,
  never float32. *Reason:* ``ARITHMETIC.md`` §7-§9.
* **P-6. Unit mechanism.** Parameterised units are ``compose.unit``s (no
  C9/C10); ``@df.unit`` only for fixed-size leaves; factories (C11) only
  where neither works, and each one recorded. *Reason:* C9/C10 are open,
  and ``compose`` already avoids both.
* **P-7. Engine swap.** An engine declares types, latency and accumulate
  order; a swap that changes the order is a different function and is
  checked against the contract reference with that order. A D-n draft, no
  core code. *Reason:* ``ip_gaps.rst`` ("narrower is a different function")
  applies to order as much as to width.
* **P-8. Wide ports.** Allo-side ports are lane arrays (``UInt(16)[N]``,
  ``UInt(16)[DIM]``); the harness packs them. *Reason:* B6/S7/S8 (no numpy
  dtype above 64 b; csim read-back of 64-bit words).
* **P-9. Unreset payload.** ``Stateful(reset=False)`` (D-14) on payload
  registers that MiniTPU leaves unreset, valids reset. *Reason:* D-14 and
  the census: MiniTPU's own semantics.
* **P-10. MiniTPU findings** (Phase 0 findings 1-4) are recorded for the
  owner and not filed upstream while the owner is away. *Reason:* filing
  was always an owner's go-ahead (checkpoint 2 items 7 and 13).
