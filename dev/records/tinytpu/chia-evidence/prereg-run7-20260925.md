## Why depth here, specifically

Breadth is the recommendation almost everywhere in this tree, and the
`T` / `MAXDIM` axis is the exception. It is the exception for three measured
reasons, not as a hedge:

1. **A candidate is 1,050.5 s at `T=8 MAXDIM=32` against 623.9 s at the
   published row** — 1.68x, and the multiplier is in the RTL rather than the
   gates. Twenty breadth variants at T=8 is 5.8 hours of machine time.
2. **The corpus narrows.** `cosim.py` asserts every dimension is a multiple of
   T, so `4x4x4` and `12x12x12` cannot run at T=8; the frozen mapper refuses
   `mlp_deep_l2` (16x16x12) for the same reason. A T=8 candidate is measured
   on **one GEMM shape and one model** instead of two and two, and `accept.py`
   reports the published-row cross-check as `NOT-COMPARABLE`.
3. **There is exactly one measured interior point**, and it is a trade rather
   than a win: 16x16x16 **674 -> 426 cycles** (-36.8 %) for **1.20 -> 2.23
   mm^2** (+85.6 %, the area proxy's estimate), clock met at 2.431 ns.

So the question here is not "which of twenty configurations is best" — the
envelope is `T ∈ [4,8] × MAXDIM ∈ [16,64]` and the interesting part is not the
enumeration. It is whether a search that *commits* can state a trade the
objective cannot state for it.

## The question

> With `T` and `MAXDIM` searchable inside the fitted envelope, does a
> depth-first worker — one line, three iterations, best-so-far — **propose a
> configuration change at all**; and if it does, does its verdict **state the
> trade** (cycles *and* area *and* the narrowed corpus) rather than reporting
> a cycle count as a win?

Two things are being measured and they are separable. First, *where the worker
looks* when the search surface has widened and nothing tells it that it has —
run 4 offered exactly this as an alternative question and it was not chosen,
so it has never been asked. Second, whether the **verdict** survives contact
with a candidate whose comparison against the published row is
`NOT-COMPARABLE` by construction, which no previous run has produced.

**Why the null is informative.** If three iterations of a committed depth
search never touch `CHIA_CONFIG`, that is run 4's finding repeated on a wider
surface, and it says the constraint is the **prompt and the spec listing, not
the search surface** — the same conclusion run 4's alternative question was
designed to reach, now with n=3 iterations behind it instead of n=1. That
finding would redirect the next quarter's effort away from widening what is
editable and toward what the agent is told is editable, which is a cheap fix
and currently an unevidenced guess.

## The prediction, and what would falsify it

- **The worker proposes a configuration change in at least one of the three
  iterations.** Weakly held. It has never happened, and the only evidence
  either way is run 4 at n=1 on a narrower surface. **Falsified by three
  iterations with `CHIA_CONFIG` untouched.**
- **If it proposes one, it proposes `T=8 MAXDIM=32`** — the one configuration
  written down in the repository's own records. That would be a **rediscovery
  by reading, not by search**, and the register below names it as such.
  **Falsified by any other interior point.** A candidate that proposes, say,
  `T=4 MAXDIM=32` — moving one axis, which nothing in the records has measured
  — is a more interesting result than one that reproduces the table.
- **No win by the scored objective.** At T=8 the scored GEMM term is
  `16x16x16` alone and the model term is `mlp_tiny` alone, so the comparison
  the objective makes is not the comparison that matters, and a "win" here
  would be an artefact of the narrowed corpus as much as of the design.
  **Falsified by an `accept.py`-confirmed win** — and if one comes back, the
  report leads with the corpus, not with the cycle count.
- **The verdict does not state area unprompted.** I predict the worker reports
  cycles and treats the resources block as a constraint to clear rather than a
  term to trade. **Falsified by an `agent_summary` that weighs the two.** This
  is the prediction whose falsification would be the best news in the run.
- **Spend under $18.** Three latency-bound sessions at $4-5, plus a control.
  **Falsified by the database figure**, never by `usage`.

## What counts as a novel design point, for this question

The series definition, unchanged: *a candidate-authored change to a unit, the
wiring or the instruction set, that beats the run's own measured control, and
is not a knob already in the tree.*

**A `CHIA_CONFIG` change does not satisfy it.** `T` and `MAXDIM` are
parameters, the envelope is fitted and published, and `T=8 MAXDIM=32` is
written down with its cycles and its area. A configuration move is a knob
flip, however large the win, and this page says so before the run rather than
after it. **This run is not expected to produce a novel design point**, and it
is not the reason to run it.

What it can produce is a **co-design point**: a pair (cycles, area) inside the
fitted envelope, measured, with its corpus named — the first thing this loop
could not state at all before `check_invariants` stopped refusing every
configuration but T=4 MAXDIM=16. That is worth having and it is a different
category. The report uses the two terms and does not blur them.

## What counts as a negative, and is reported as a result

- **`CHIA_CONFIG` never touched.** The headline, with the trace showing
  whether the worker ever read the parameter set or the envelope.
- **A configuration proposed and refused by the envelope.** Which refusal
  fired (`area_proxy.envelope_refusals`, stage `config`) is a result about
  where the fitted boundary sits against what an agent reaches for.
- **A configuration that dies at `gate:param`.** The candidate built at the
  proposed configuration and not at the others it is rebuilt at.
- **A cycle improvement whose area the report cannot defend.** Recorded as a
  trade, stated as a trade, and not called a win.
- **The cross-check reading `NOT-COMPARABLE`.** Expected at T != 4, not a
  stop, and reported with `shapes_skipped` and `model_skipped` naming what
  dropped out.
- **The cross-check reading `DISAGREES` at T=4.** A stop, not a datum.

## Rediscovery register — a win that is any of these is not a finding

1. **`T=8 MAXDIM=32`.** Measured and published in
   `dev/records/tinytpu/codesign-space-20260925.rst`: 674 -> 426 cycles, 1.20
   -> 2.23 mm^2, 2.431 ns. A candidate that proposes it has read the records.
2. **`TPU_DMA_WIDEN` / `_WIDEN` defaulting on**, and note that the widening is
   worth **-287 / -583 at `MAXDIM=64`** against **zero** on the model term at
   the scored configuration. A candidate that moves MAXDIM *and* widens the
   burst is claiming two effects, and the report separates them with a $0
   follow-up before crediting either, exactly as run 3 did.
3. **The `ac2sp` channel depth**, and **`QD`**.

Kept in this file, **not in the prompt**.

## Stop rules

1. **The cumulative or per-run cap refuses.** The ceiling is not raised.
2. **The control disagrees with the published row at T=4.** A stop.
3. **`smoke.py` fails.**
4. **A `tamper` verdict on a tree that should have been clean.**
5. **Two consecutive iterations spend their whole `score_cycles` budget on a
   candidate that dies at `config` or `gate:param`.** The envelope, not the
   search, is the subject; report it and stop rather than buying a third.
6. **An iteration exceeds 2,400 s with no verdict twice.** At T=8 a single
   candidate can consume the session; that is a fact about the timeout against
   the candidate cost, it is worth reporting once, and it is not worth paying
   for twice.

## Deviation reporting

Any departure from the plan above — a different worker or iteration count, a
different cap, a restart after a kill, a hand-edited spec, a second
`smoke.py`, a control measured at a configuration other than the scored one,
an objective that moved under the run, or a question changed after the first
call — is named in the appended result section with what it was and why. There
is no resume: a restart is a new run, a new run directory and a new index row.
If the plan is departed from before any paid call, this page is edited and
re-pushed to `main` instead, and that edit is itself noted.
